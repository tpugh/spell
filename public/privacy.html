Spell privacy

Spell is spelling practice for young learners. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no servers of its own. The app and the Chrome extension are published by the Spell project on GitHub.

What we collect

Nothing. Spell does not collect personal information, analytics, or usage data.

Where your data lives

  • Web app: word lists and test results are stored in your browser's IndexedDB and never leave the device.
  • Chrome extension: the same data is stored with chrome.storage.local on your device. The extension requests only the storage permission.
  • Text-to-speech: Spell asks your browser or operating system to read words aloud. Those speech services are provided by your platform and may follow their own privacy terms; Spell itself does not send your data anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete word lists and test results inside the app, clear site data in your browser, or uninstall the extension. Removing the extension deletes its local storage.
